Scoring: Each five-point scale gives 10 for exact, 8 for the same side, 3 or 1 for neutral versus moderate/extreme, and 0 across opposite sides. Workweek and human-needs choices are worth 10 for an exact match. For professions, each rank earns 10 for the exact job in the correct place, 6 for that job in another position, or 1 for a different job in the same profession category. Maximum: 100.
